# HealthLens AI 🩺

### Intelligent Medical Report Analyzer & Personalized Health Insight System

**HealthLens AI** is an intelligent health analysis platform designed to help users understand their medical reports, monitor health parameters, identify unusual laboratory patterns, and track changes in their health over time.

The platform transforms complex medical test results into clear, easy-to-understand insights, helping users become more aware of their health and have informed discussions with healthcare professionals.

## ✨ Key Features

* **📄 Medical Report Analysis:** Upload and analyze medical reports to view laboratory parameters, values, and reference ranges.
* **🤖 AI-Powered Health Insights:** Receive simplified explanations of medical results and personalized insights based on available health information.
* **🧠 Anomaly Detection:** Identify unusual patterns in laboratory results using machine learning for additional awareness.
* **📊 Health Track Record:** Monitor changes in health parameters over time through historical records and visual charts.
* **👤 Personalized Health Profile:** Maintain relevant health details, lifestyle information, and medical history.
* **📈 Health Insights Dashboard:** View health summaries, parameter trends, and areas that may require attention.
* **📑 PDF Reports:** Export organized health analysis summaries for personal reference and discussion with healthcare professionals.

## 🎯 Objective

The goal of HealthLens AI is to make medical information more understandable and accessible by combining report analysis, machine learning-based pattern detection, and AI-generated explanations in one platform.

## 🛠️ Technology

* **Frontend:** React, Vite
* **Backend:** Python, FastAPI
* **Database:** SQLite
* **AI:** Google Gemini
* **Machine Learning:** Isolation Forest

## ⚠️ Medical Disclaimer

HealthLens AI is intended for educational and informational purposes only. It does not provide medical diagnoses, replace professional medical advice, or substitute for consultation with a qualified healthcare professional. Always discuss concerning results with a healthcare professional.

## 🚀 Vision

To help users better understand their health information, recognize changes in their laboratory results, and take a more informed approach to conversations about their health.
